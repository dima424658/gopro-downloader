Connect Your Camera over Bluetooth

Turn on your camera and open its device pairing screen so the app can find it:

  • On GoPro models with a touchscreen, open the dashboard, go to Connections, then choose Connect Device or Pair Device.
  • If the camera asks for a connection type, choose GoPro Quik. On models with a settings menu, the path is usually Preferences → Connections → Connect Device → GoPro Quik.

Menu names and locations vary by model and firmware. Leave the camera on its pairing screen, return here, click the refresh icon, and select your GoPro. Do not pair the camera through your operating system’s Bluetooth settings.

COHN, which connects the camera to your home Wi-Fi network for HTTPS access, is supported on HERO12 Black, HERO13 Black, MAX 2, Mission 1, and Mission 1 Pro. Open GoPro requires compatible firmware, and feature availability varies by model.
